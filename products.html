Zentro Inventory

Operations Live now

Powerful stock management and inventory control software. It bills a customer, moves the stock, costs it against the purchase that actually supplied it and posts the double entry — in one action, on your own server.

Replaces: a billing app, a stock register and a spreadsheet your accountant reconciles every March.

Visit the product site Pricing

What it does today

  • GST invoicing with tax stored per line
  • Point of sale with shifts and cash-up
  • FIFO costing, layered per purchase
  • Double-entry ledger behind every document
  • Per-warehouse stock and transfers
  • Batch, expiry and serial tracking
  • Barcode labels — A4 sheets or thermal rolls
  • 14 report groups, CSV and PDF export
  • Receivables, payables and ageing
  • Scheduled backups and one-click restore
